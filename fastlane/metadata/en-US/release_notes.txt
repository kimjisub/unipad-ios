- Fixed a crash that could close the app while playing, when switching autoplay or practice modes.
- Launchpads running the Launchpad Core Firmware (CoreFW) are now supported, and the Launchpad selection screen was redesigned.
- Matrix and Mystrix devices are recognised on both old and new firmware.
- Opening a shared pack link now asks before anything is downloaded.
- Legacy packs with Korean file names play their sounds again, and packs that load on Android now load the same way here.
- A pack's backing track is no longer cut off after many pad hits.
- If your bookmarks and play counts cannot be loaded, the app now tells you instead of silently not saving them.
- Theme previews no longer get cut off on iPad.
