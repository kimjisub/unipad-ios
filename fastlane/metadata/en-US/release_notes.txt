Songs now wait for you. After you switch apps, cover the play screen or lose sound, press Play to continue. Your position and practice mode are kept.

Dragging across pads is now optional and off by default. If you play that way, turn on "Slide between pads" in the play menu or Settings.

Also in this update:
- Pads right under the top edge of the screen respond at once instead of after a delay. While playing, the first swipe from the top or bottom edge goes to the pads; swipe again to reach the system.
- Sounds: Ogg Vorbis and Ogg Opus sounds play, including Opus inside M4A files. A pad press no longer waits on a sound that has just finished repeating.
- Packs with very long or very large sounds now open; only the sounds over the limit stay silent. The play screen tells you how many sounds were left out and why. If none of a pack's sounds can play, it says so instead of quietly going back to the list. AIFF, CAF and A-law/μ-law WAV sounds are listed as unreadable.
- Lights start from the moment you press a pad, and each pad's sound and lights end on their own.
- Picking a chain wins over a chain jump that is still pending.
- Guide and step practice keep notes you haven't played yet, including after you pause and play again. Pausing a guide during its last wait no longer ends the practice, and switching from Autoplay to Guide no longer guides a note that already played.
- Stopping Autoplay leaves the pad you are still holding lit and sounding until you lift your finger.
- Auto Mapping keeps pads that play together, and you can restore the previous autoplay from the menu.
- Launchpad and MIDI: presses, releases and lights are read the same way on every supported model; your device stays connected when the list refreshes; it keeps working after you return to UniPad or close a pack that is still loading; and its buttons no longer change settings when a pack finishes loading while UniPad is in the background.
- Importing: large packs import without running out of memory, files outside the pack are left out, ZIP64 archives import, and an archive with several packs says so.
- More packs open: an info file that lists a setting twice uses the last value, and sound names that differ only in accented-letter encoding are found.
- Store downloads resume after a dropped connection and keep going when you reopen the store.
- iPad: back buttons stay clear of the window controls and the status bar, and long titles get their own line in narrow windows.
- Packs without a title show their folder name, and the theme logo no longer overlaps a wide chain row.
- Open source notices are now in Settings.
- Starting with this version, UniPad can show "Update available" when a newer version is on the App Store. You will first see it when the version after this one comes out.
