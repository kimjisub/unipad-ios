- The Launchpad selection screen shows connection status and what to try next, with help tailored to the current state. It stays open when a device is missing, cannot be opened, has an unknown model, or disconnects.
- Play continues when a disconnected audio output is replaced by another external output. If only the device speaker remains, AutoPlay pauses and playing sounds stop; tap Play to resume AutoPlay.

- Themes without a version in theme.json now import successfully and show version 1.0.
- "Create your own theme" opens the theme creation guide, in Korean when the app is set to Korean and in English otherwise.
