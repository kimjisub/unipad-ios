Getting started and your library
- Find your first UniPack with guidance in an empty library.
- See clearer pack details after importing and use Play now to open that pack.
- Download and import from the home cards, with duplicate toolbar icons removed.
- Find Settings by name and see recently played packs on the home screen.
- Search the loaded library without rereading every pack folder as you type. Library and pack-detail reads run in the background.
- Keep selected-pack play counts and last-played details current after returning from play.
- Deleting a pack clears its saved play history and bookmark after successful deletion, updates library totals, and shows deletion errors. Reimporting that pack starts fresh.

Importing and downloading
- Show a warning when an imported pack is missing sound files.
- Keep import progress visible until the result is ready and prevent an earlier success from replacing a later failure.
- Protect separate downloads and imports from sharing installation folders or removing each other's files.
- Write pack downloads in chunks and clean up partial files after cancelled or failed transfers finish.
- Ask about notifications on the first store download instead of at app launch. Downloads do not wait for permission; unanswered permission checks can be retried.
- Point the update link to the current UniPad App Store page.

Playing and lights
- Fix app hangs when stopping repeated sounds, leaving play, or changing step-practice mode. Preserve the order of AutoPlay sounds, lights, and chain changes.
- Fix a crash when playback helpers are released.
- Keep endless light animations running past a processing limit and turn lights off on release.
- Reject malformed light commands and limit out-of-range Launchpad brightness values.
- Center the pad grid while keeping chains clear of the menu, and keep the theme background steady while a pack opens.

Language and settings
- Follow the device language and translate more device-status, pack-count, and community text.
- Explain the pad tap-order number setting and its required play-screen switch.
- Enlarge the Settings back button's touch area.

Stability
- Ensure the build includes the official protection against a server-data key that can cause an app crash.
